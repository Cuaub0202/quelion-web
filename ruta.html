How it works

Three steps. Zero behavior change for your drivers.

01

Driver sends a message — any message

Your driver texts "Ya salí de la planta, voy a Monterrey" on WhatsApp or Telegram. No form, no app, no protocol — just a normal message. Ruta reads it, extracts the shipment update, and logs the milestone automatically.

02

Dashboard updates in real time

Your coordinator sees the milestone update instantly — no data entry required. The ops dashboard shows every active shipment, its current stage, and flags any shipment that hasn't reported in over 2 hours.

03

Client gets notified automatically

When a shipment is delivered, the client receives a notification immediately — without the coordinator having to remember to send it. Each client has a configurable notification level: from silent (ops-only) to VIP (full updates at every milestone).

Features

Built for freight coordinators, not software teams.

WhatsApp & Telegram AI agent

Understands natural-language messages in Spanish and English. Extracts milestones from text, voice notes, and audio messages. No scripts, no keywords — drivers communicate naturally.

Real-time operations dashboard

Every active shipment in one view. Filter by status, milestone, or alert level. See which drivers haven't reported, which shipments are at risk, and which are on track — at a glance.

Automatic follow-ups & escalations

If a driver hasn't reported in 2 hours, Ruta sends a follow-up automatically. If there's still no response, it escalates to the coordinator. Your team handles exceptions — Ruta handles the routine.

Client tracking & notifications

Clients receive automatic updates at key milestones. Each client has a configurable notification level. Share a live tracking link via WhatsApp — no app download required on their end.

Multi-tenant team management

Invite coordinators, operators, and managers with role-based access. Each team member sees only what they need. Audit log of every action for accountability.

CSV import & analytics

Import active shipments from any spreadsheet in seconds. Analytics on operations, AI costs, driver response rates, and weekly AI-generated insights on your freight patterns.

FAQ

Common questions

Do my drivers need to install a new app? +

No. Drivers keep using WhatsApp or Telegram exactly as they do today. Ruta understands their messages automatically — no training, no new app, no behavior change required.

Does Ruta replace my TMS? +

No. Ruta adds the communication layer that most TMS systems lack. Import orders via CSV and Ruta handles driver communication and client notifications on top of your existing workflow.

What if the AI misunderstands a driver message? +

Every action Ruta takes is visible in the operations dashboard. Your coordinators can review, override, or correct any milestone update in real time. The AI assists — your team stays in control.

Which messaging channels does Ruta support? +

Ruta works with WhatsApp and Telegram — the two channels most freight teams in Latin America already use. You sell coordination automation, not a specific messaging app.

How long does setup take? +

Most teams are operational within one business day. Connect your WhatsApp or Telegram number, import your active shipments via CSV, and Ruta starts handling communication immediately.
