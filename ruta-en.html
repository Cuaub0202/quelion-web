The problem

Status on the Mexico leg lives in someone's WhatsApp.

«¿Ya saliste?», «¿cómo vas?», «¿ya cruzaste?». Someone on your desk writes every one of those messages, in Spanish, and the answers stay in a chat nobody else sees. Ruta asks those questions for your team and keeps the replies in the history of the load.

How it works

Your drivers keep texting the way they already do.

01

The driver answers as usual

A text like «ya salí de la planta, voy a Nuevo Laredo» on WhatsApp or Telegram. No forms and no app. Ruta understands the message and matches it to the load.

02

Status changes follow fixed rules

The AI reads the message and a fixed rule decides whether the status change goes through. Every change is logged with its time, and your coordinator can correct the status of a load from the dashboard.

03

If nobody answers, Ruta follows up and escalates

If a driver misses a check-in at a given stage, Ruta writes again during operating hours. If there is still no answer, it alerts your coordinator. Timing is set per stage.

FAQ

Common questions

Do drivers need to install anything? +

No. They keep using WhatsApp or Telegram. Ruta messages them in Spanish and understands their text replies.

Is the dashboard in English? +

Not yet. The operations dashboard is in Spanish today. Drivers get their messages in Spanish, and so do the alerts to your desk.

Does Ruta replace my TMS? +

No. Ruta handles the conversation with the driver and the status of each load. Active loads come in from a CSV file.

Does it use GPS? +

No. The status of each load comes from what the driver reports and from the follow-up rules for each stage.

How much does it cost? +

It depends on how many loads you run per month. We quote on the call, in USD, before setting anything up.
