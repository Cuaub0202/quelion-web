Status on every load lives in someone's WhatsApp.

“¿Ya saliste?”, “¿cómo vas?”, “¿a qué hora llegas?”. Someone on your team writes every one of those messages, in Spanish, and the answers stay in a chat nobody else sees. Ruta asks those questions for your team and keeps the replies in the history of the load.

Your drivers keep texting the way they already do.

01

The driver answers as usual

A text like “ya crucé, voy para Monterrey” or a voice note on WhatsApp or Telegram. No forms and no app. Ruta understands the message and matches it to the load.

02

Status changes follow fixed rules

The AI reads the message and a fixed rule decides whether the status change goes through. Every change is logged with its time, and your coordinator can correct the status of a load from the dashboard.

03

If nobody answers, Ruta follows up and escalates

If a driver misses a check-in at a given stage, Ruta writes again during the hours you set, daytime or around the clock. If there is still no answer, it alerts your team and, if nobody acknowledges the alert, calls whoever is on call. Timing is set per stage.

Common questions

Do drivers need to install anything? +

No. They keep using WhatsApp or Telegram. Ruta messages them in Spanish and understands their Spanish replies, in text or voice notes.

Is the dashboard in English? +

It's in Spanish, like the driver messages and the alerts to your team: the language your drivers and carriers work in. From Claude or ChatGPT you can ask about your loads in English.

Does Ruta replace my TMS? +

No, it works alongside it. Ruta handles the conversation with the driver and the status of each load. Loads come in from your TMS export, by email with the order PDF, or from your AI through the connector.

Does it use GPS? +

Yes, when your carrier shares read-only access to its GPS: Ruta reads the truck's position, detects stops and detours, and only asks when something looks off. Without it, the status of each load comes from what the driver reports and from the follow-up rules for each stage.

What does Ruta do today? +

Ruta messages your drivers on WhatsApp or Telegram, in Spanish, whether they're your own drivers, a carrier's drivers or owner-operators, and understands their replies, voice notes included. With read-only access to the truck's GPS, yours or the carrier's, it asks the driver when something looks off. If a driver goes quiet, it follows up. If there's still no answer, it alerts your team and, if nobody acknowledges the alert, calls whoever's on call, then their backup. Drivers only get messages, never calls. If you turn it on, it updates your customer on WhatsApp, in Spanish, when the truck departs, crosses, arrives and delivers, with a link to follow the load. Each stage has its own hours, daytime or 24/7, and your team tracks every load on a dashboard.

Loads come in from your Excel or CSV file, the dashboard, the order PDF emailed to your Ruta address, or Claude or ChatGPT through the Ruta MCP connector, which also lets you check on your loads, fix an appointment or pause follow-up. Ruta talks one-to-one with each driver, not in group chats.

How much does it cost? +

It depends on how many loads you run per month. After the call we send you a proposal with the price, in USD, before setting anything up. Setup is quoted separately, in the same proposal.
